This service does not remove or collect dead birds

This service is for monitoring disease in wild birds, such as bird flu. It is not a service for having dead birds taken away. We only collect a small number of birds for testing.

How to get a dead bird removed

  • on your own property, you can dispose of it yourself — do not touch it with your bare hands
  • on a road, pavement or public land, contact your local council (opens in new tab)

Read the guidance on disposing of dead wild birds safely (opens in new tab).

Still want to help monitor disease?

Even if a bird will not be collected, telling us about it still helps us track disease. Report a dead wild bird.
