How to dispose of the bird safely

If the bird is on your property, you can dispose of it. Do not touch it with your bare hands.

  • use disposable gloves, or a plastic bag turned inside out over your hand
  • place the bird in a plastic bag, then in a second bag
  • tie the bags and put them in your normal household waste (outside bin)
  • wash your hands thoroughly afterwards
