This service does not cover Northern Ireland

This service is for reporting dead wild birds in England, Scotland and Wales.

In Northern Ireland, dead wild birds are handled by the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs (DAERA).

  • report it to the DAERA dead wild bird helpline (opens in new tab)
  • do not touch the bird with your bare hands

If the bird is in England, Scotland or Wales, start again and choose the right country.
