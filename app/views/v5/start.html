Report dead wild birds

How to report dead wild birds.

https://www.gov.uk/guidance/report-dead-wild-birds

Use this service to report dead wild birds that may have died from disease, including bird flu.

Your report helps us understand how diseases are spreading.

We may collect and test reported birds for bird flu or other diseases.

If you’re under 18, ask a parent or guardian to fill in this form.

{{ govukButton({ text: "Start now", href: basePath + "/reporting-dead-bird", isStartButton: true }) }}

You can also report dead wild birds by phone: 03459 33 55 77 (find out about call charges).

{{ govukWarningText({ text: "Do not touch dead birds", iconFallbackText: "Warning" }) }}

If you want to know how to dispose of dead wild birds or ask for them to be removed, read the guidance on removing and disposing of dead wild birds.

If you have found a dead wild bird and suspect it has died as a result of:

  • wildlife crime, you must report them to the police
  • poisoning from pesticides or rodenticides, call the Wildlife Incident Investigation Scheme (WISS) on 0800 321 600 (calls are free)

If you’ve found a sick or injured bird, follow the guidance for reporting other dead, sick or injured animals.
