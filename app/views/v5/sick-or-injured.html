Sick or injured birds

This service is to report dead wild birds that may have died from disease, including bird flu.

If you’ve found a sick or injured bird, follow the guidance for reporting other dead, sick or injured animals.

If you keep poultry or captive birds and suspect bird flu, read guidance on the signs of bird flu and how to report it.
