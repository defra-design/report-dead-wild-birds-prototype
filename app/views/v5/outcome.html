What happens next

We’ll use your report to decide if we need to collect the bird for testing.

If we collect the bird:

  • we’ll usually collect the bird within 48 hours, but it can take up to 4 days
  • we may contact you to confirm the location
  • if we test the bird, we’ll email you the results within 2 to 4 weeks

If we do not collect the bird within 4 working days, or you cannot wait for collection, read the guidance on how to dispose of dead wild birds or ask for them to be removed.

Why your report is important

Your report helps us understand where wild birds are dying and which species are affected.

This information supports our wild bird surveillance programme and helps us investigate new and emerging threats to wild birds.

Find out about our wild bird surveillance programme.

Risks to humans from bird flu

The risk of bird flu passing from infected dead birds to humans is low. You can read NHS guidance on the risks to humans from bird flu.

If you’re concerned about symptoms, phone the NHS on 111.
