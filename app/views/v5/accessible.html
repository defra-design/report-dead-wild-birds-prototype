Can the bird be reached safely, without tools or equipment, if we need to collect it?

Answer “No” if the bird is:

  • in water, or can only be reached by boat
  • somewhere difficult to reach, like on a roof or in a tree
  • behind a barrier, fence or locked area
  • in a location that would be dangerous to access, such as a railway line or motorway
