Can the bird be reached safely, without tools or equipment?

Answer ‘No’ if the bird is:
  • in water and can only be reached by boat
  • somewhere that would need a ladder, like on a roof or in a tree
  • behind a barrier, fence or locked area (unless it is on your property)
  • in a location that would be dangerous to access, such as a railway line or motorway
