What happens next

We will collect the body of the bird within the next 48 hours, subject to the availability of operators collecting in your region.

Bird on private land

If the bird is on your property, we’ll try to contact you to arrange a pick-up time. You can bag and store the bird for us to collect (if you can do this, follow the directions for bagging birds in removing and disposing of dead wild birds and do not store the bird indoors). Please leave the bag in an obvious and accessible place if you’re not at home.

Bird on public land

If the bird is on public land, we may need to contact you to confirm the location.

If we test the bird

If we test the bird, we’ll email you the test results within 2 to 4 weeks.

Find out about our wild bird surveillance programme.

Bird not collected

If we do not collect the bird and you want to know how to dispose of dead wild birds on your property or ask for them to be removed from public land, read the guidance on removing and disposing of dead wild birds.
