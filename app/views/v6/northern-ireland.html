Reporting dead wild birds in Northern Ireland

This service covers England, Scotland and Wales. It looks like the bird you found is in Northern Ireland, which has its own reporting service.

To report a dead wild bird in Northern Ireland, contact the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs (DAERA) and read the guidance on reporting dead wild birds in Northern Ireland.

Do not touch the bird. Your report to DAERA helps them monitor the spread of bird flu and other diseases.
