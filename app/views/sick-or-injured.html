This service is for dead birds only

You told us the bird is sick or injured, not dead. This service is used to monitor disease in birds that have died, so we cannot take a report about a live bird here.

What you can do

  • for a sick or injured wild bird, contact the RSPCA (opens in new tab) or a local wildlife rescue
  • do not touch the bird with your bare hands
  • if you keep poultry or captive birds and suspect bird flu, report it under the bird flu rules (opens in new tab)

If the bird later dies and you still know where it is, you can start a new report.
